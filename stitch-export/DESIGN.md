## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes Swiss Graphic Design (International Typographic Style), research-lab rigor, and modernist industrial product clarity. It serves an avant-garde Creative Technologist and Brand Designer whose practice lives at the intersection of generative engineering, architectural systems, and visual identity.

### Visual Philosophy & Influences
- **Swiss Precision (Josef Müller-Brockmann / Pentagram):** Mathematical grid discipline, asymmetry, rigorous vertical alignments, and hairline divisions that treat whitespace as structural matter.
- **Experimental Computation (MIT Media Lab):** Technical metadata overlays, monospaced computational telemetry, data-dense indexes, and raw architectural documentation.
- **Hardware Minimalism (Apple):** Laser-focused restraint, reduction of visual ornamentation, seamless micro-interactions, and pristine typographic hierarchy.

### Atmospheric Profile
- **Tone:** Intellectual, calculated, authoritative, experimental.
- **Structure:** Absolute geometric discipline via 1px hairline rules, clear modular zones, and intentional negative space.
- **Contrast:** Dramatic shifts between massive editorial display type and minute, high-density monospaced data tags.

## Colors

The palette operates on high-contrast monochrome foundations with an energetic, hyper-precise electric blue accent reserved for states, system telemetry, and functional interactive anchors.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0A0A0A`):** Obsidian black. Governs principal typography, structural boundaries, hairline grids, and dominant interactive elements.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`):** Electric Royal Blue. Used selectively for active links, technical indices, real-time status pulses, and system highlights. It never dominates backgrounds; it acts as a signal flare.
- **Tertiary (`#64748B`):** Muted Slate. Designated for secondary metadata, figure labels, and muted structural guidelines.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFFFF` & `#F8FAFC`):** The primary visual ground. Crisp pure white for cards and modal surfaces; Slate-50 for viewport canvas foundation.
- **Hairline Rule (`#E2E8F0` / `#0A0A0A14`):** Engineered 1px partition color maintaining geometric clarity without optical heaviness.

## Typography

The typographic engine pairs high-density neutral grotesk shapes with technical monospaced telemetry.

### Typographic Directives
- **Proportional Discipline:** Display headlines utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) and compact line heights to create architectural typographic blocks reminiscent of Swiss exhibition posters.
- **Dual-Register Tension:** Contrast body prose and headlines (`Inter`) against rigorous informational metadata (`JetBrains Mono`). All project specifications, dates, technical taxonomies, index counts, and geographic coordinates must render strictly in `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Case System:** Labels and tags leverage uppercase monospaced type with expanded tracking (`0.06em`) to establish authoritative cataloging.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are anchored to a mathematical 12-column grid visible through persistent or contextual hairline grid guides.

### Grid & Modular Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid. Outer margins set at `4rem` (`64px`), columns separated by `2rem` (`32px`) gutters. Section headers and visual artifacts align strictly along vertical axis increments.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacks vertically while maintaining visible horizontal hairline row separators.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` scaled across multiples) balance dense project documentation grids. Whitespace is never accidental; it mirrors the structural void of physical architectural drawings.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects heavy skeuomorphism and blurry drop shadows in favor of modernist flatness, dimensional hairlines, and high-precision tonal stacking.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Structural Planarity:** Depth is expressed entirely through 1px crisp hairlines (`#0A0A0A` at 10% opacity in light mode, or `#E2E8F0`). Borders delineate hierarchy rather than floating shadows.
- **Frosted Optical Substrates:** Sticky navigation headers and overlay panels employ subtle glassmorphic blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with an 85% translucent neutral fill (`#FFFFFFD9`), evoking precision optical lenses.
- **Micro-Shadow Layering:** When elevation is mandatory (such as floating contextual inspectors or active popovers), apply an ultra-diffused, sharp ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.06)`. No colored or glowing shadows are permitted outside of technical blue laser indicators.

## Shapes

The system relies strictly on unrounded geometry (`roundedness: 0`), reflecting pure architectural constructivism, Swiss grid alignment, and industrial device screens.

### Shape Directives
- **Zero Radius (`0px`):** Buttons, images, portfolio thumbnails, tag labels, input containers, and modal dialogues maintain razor-sharp right angles.
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards and modules feature explicit 1px borders without outer radiuses, creating seamless modular matrices when placed edge-to-edge.
- **Micro Indicators:** Status dots and live node pulses are the sole exceptions permitted to use circular geometry (`50%` radius), providing functional contrast against the surrounding orthogonal grid.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid obsidian rectangle (`#0A0A0A`), crisp white typography in `JetBrains Mono` or medium-weight `Inter`, 0px border radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover state transitions background instantaneously or via an abrupt 100ms cut to electric blue (`#2563EB`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px solid obsidian border (`#0A0A0A`), obsidian typography. Hover: invert to solid obsidian fill with pure white label.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure text accompanied by an inline directional arrow (`→`) in `JetBrains Mono`. On hover, the arrow translates 4px horizontally with an electric blue accent change.

### Monospace Spec Chips & Data Tags
- **Appearance:** Low-profile rectangular pills with sharp corners. 1px hairline border in `#E2E8F0`, transparent or `#F8FAFC` background.
- **Typography:** `meta-tag` level (`JetBrains Mono`, uppercase, tracked out).
- **Structure:** Optional leading dot indicator (`#2563EB`) indicating project state (e.g., `[● IN PRODUCTION]`, `[SYS.2024]`, `[WebGL / GLSL]`).

### Data Tables & Project Lists
- **Structure:** Edge-to-edge modular rows separated by horizontal 1px hairline rules (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Hover Transition:** Rows highlight subtly to `#F8FAFC` while changing the index counter color to `#2563EB`.
- **Columns:** Standard alignment across 4 metadata columns: Index (`01`), Project Title, Domain/Discipline, Year.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Controls:** Minimalist underline or 1px bounding frame with sharp corners. Transparent background, sharp typography. Focus shifts hairline border to 1px `#2563EB` without blurry focus rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square indicators (0px radius) with 1px black borders. Active checkbox fills solid black with a sharp white checkmark or inner solid square.

### Portfolio Cards & Project Frames
- **Image Treatment:** Aspect ratios strictly locked (16:9, 4:3, or 1:1) within hairline border enclosures. Media renders in natural or high-contrast duotone, revealing full saturation upon cursor focus.
- **Caption System:** Captions sit below the image enclosure separated by an uninterrupted horizontal hairline, pairing an `Inter` headline with right-aligned `JetBrains Mono` client and year metadata.

### System Metadata Banner (Specialized Component)
- **Telemetry Bar:** Fixed or inline persistent strip exhibiting viewport dimensions, local time coordinates (UTC/EST), and live FPS/Canvas status to reinforce the MIT Media Lab computational rigor.
